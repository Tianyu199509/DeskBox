# DeskBox 1.5.9

The polish release. The file right-click menus move to the Windows 11-native shape — a single column of inline-icon rows in Explorer's classic order on acrylic — with an "Open with" submenu, "New"/"View"/"Sort by" submenus, and native ZIP support; namespace shortcuts inherit their target's verbs, and the Recycle Bin shortcut inside a box doubles as a delete target. A wiped configuration now triggers a one-time recovery offer instead of a silent reset to defaults. Korean joins the language roster alongside a full translation-quality pass over all twelve existing languages. The first-run experience is redesigned with a logo assembly animation and a file-drop scene, Shell locations (Recycle Bin, This PC) drop into boxes as shortcuts, and under the hood widget placement survives display-topology changes via a deliberate-placement timestamp arbitration, edge-docked bars no longer jump to a corner on reveal, group-tab capsules expand promptly after a member swap, and the idle-memory reclaim returns to the "five seconds after you stop touching it" behavior.

Scope note: the System Monitor widget under development on `feat/system-monitor-widget` is **not** part of this release; every non-monitor change from that line ships here.

Release date: 2026-10-23 (content prepared from the `release/1.5.9` branch — all non-monitor work from `feat/system-monitor-widget`, rebased onto `main`).

## Highlights

- **A recovery offer after a configuration wipe.** A wiped or corrupted profile used to come back silently as factory defaults while perfectly good automatic backups sat in the snapshot folder. When a launch now detects missing settings plus a surviving snapshot that passes the hard gates — unique, schema-valid, not a first-run leftover, with real widgets, so a garbage snapshot cannot hijack the prompt — the first interactive activation raises a one-time window showing the backup's date and widget count. "Restore from backup" confirms in place and lands through a two-phase transactional restart; "Start fresh", or simply closing the window, starts clean and retires the question permanently. While the decision is open, scheduled automatic snapshots stay paused (manual "back up now" is untouched and cannot push the pending backup out of retention), a notification repeats at most once per app run, and the Backup & Restore page now discloses that snapshots in a custom backup folder are never auto-offered after a wipe.
- **Korean language support.** The interface, installer, and first-run experience ship in Korean alongside the existing 12 languages, plus a full translation-quality pass over the 1.3.8-era machine translations in es/ar/ru/hi/bn/fr and the other locales.
- **A calmer, more capable updater.** Daily wall-clock-anchored checks with failure back-off, actionable update-toast buttons, automatic release notes after an update, background downloads that skip metered connections with a ready toast, a stage-following status badge, version navigation across the full published history in the update log (identical on every machine, offline-readable once viewed), and an in-flight activity card in settings; background download and update notifications get separate switches.
- **A clear notice on fully elevated desktops.** A one-time startup notice explains why dragging out of widgets is unavailable when the whole desktop runs as administrator (UAC off, built-in Administrator, or company-managed), with the exact way back for Pro, Home, and alternate accounts, a copyable command, and a toast fallback; the drag-drop diagnostics attribute the same, and an acknowledged notice never returns.
- **The tray icon can be hidden.** A "Hidden" tray icon style: no icon at startup, immediate runtime switching, no revival on taskbar recreation.
- **Windows 11-native right-click menus.** A single column of inline-icon rows in Explorer's classic order, rendered directly on acrylic; "Open with" lists real associated apps including UWP with icons; "New"/"View"/"Sort by" submenus on the blank area; native ZIP compress (tar variants, multi-select) and extract. Namespace shortcuts (This PC, Recycle Bin, Control Panel) inherit their target's verbs — "Empty Recycle Bin" on the bin's own shortcut (greyed out when empty), "Create shortcut" everywhere.
- **The Recycle Bin shortcut in a box accepts drops as deletes.** Files dragged onto it — from the desktop, Explorer, or another box — go to the Recycle Bin with Windows' own confirmation, and the shortcut's empty/full icon updates right after a drop or empty.
- **Shell locations drop into boxes as shortcuts.** Recycle Bin, This PC, and similar Explorer locations become working shortcuts when dropped on a box, with toast feedback for empty or busy sources.
- **A redesigned first-run experience.** Three-piece logo assembly animation, a file-drop scene, and the real managed storage path with move/paste hints.
- **Steadier widgets.** Placement survives display-topology changes (a stale snapshot can no longer overwrite the user's latest placement), edge-docked bars stop jumping to a corner on reveal, group-tab capsules expand promptly on re-entry, the minimum expanded size halves to 25 DIP, and revealing shortly after hiding is prompt again (the post-hide trim waits and skips small gains).
- **No more color-submenu crash on Windows 11 25H2.** Right-clicking a todo item and hovering into the color-marker submenu no longer terminates the app: the unselected color dot is drawn as font-free geometry instead of a glyph pinned to a font whose file 25H2 removed while leaving its registration behind, which made the menu's first measure fail fatally.

## Validation

- Full test suite (`tests/DeskBox.Tests`, x64): **5803/5803 passed** on 2026-10-23 (final tree = release/1.5.9 merge plus the tray/elevation refinements, the version-rail consistency fix, and the todo color-submenu crash fix); the AOT retail publish gate passes with no new warning codes (the two new ones from the updater and name-expansion batches were fixed at source).
- The wipe-recovery offer was reviewed by four parallel agents, hardened (candidate gates, snapshot-pause with retention pin, race guards), and exercised live across seven machine scenarios: zero-disturb, wipe→restore, fresh start, boot/autostart deferral with pause reminder, corrupt-settings arm, custom-folder disclosure, and close-as-fresh-start. The recycle-bin drop target landed with contract tests and a first-drag fix verified in live logs; the menu migration re-pinned its AOT audit contracts.
- The todo color-submenu crash on Win11 25H2 (26300) was root-caused from a field diagnostics package plus a debugger-trapped local repro (stowed-exception decode: CFontFamily::GetTextLineBoundsMetrics failing on the removed-but-registered Segoe UI Symbol font), fixed with font-free geometry, and re-verified live on the affected machine.
- The updater experience batch passed two Simon acceptance rounds on the developer machine (version-downgrade live runs); the tray-icon "Hidden" style passed its live matrix A–E.
- The reveal-jump incident was reproduced, root-caused via verbose/perf traces, fixed, and re-verified live on the developer machine across F7 hide/show cycles and restarts.
- The group-tab hover delay was reproduced with performance traces, fixed in two layers (urgent warm-up + hold ceiling), and confirmed improved on the developer machine; a residual single-frame stutter during the switch morph is logged as known minor debt.
- The "Open with" empty-list and popup-menu failures from the Win10 VM acceptance round were fixed via a full COM vtable rewrite and re-verified in the VM.

### 主要更新

- **配置清空后的恢复提议。** 此前配置被清空或损坏时，DeskBox 会默默以默认设置回来，而完好的自动备份仍躺在快照目录里。现在启动检测到设置缺失且存在通过硬门校验的幸存快照（唯一、schema 合法、非首启残留、含真实格子——垃圾快照无法劫持询问）时，首次交互激活弹出一次性询问窗，列明备份时间与格子数量。「从备份恢复」当场确认、经两段式事务重启落地；「全新开始」或直接关窗即选择干净开始，此后不再追问。决议未落定期间定时自动快照保持暂停（手动“立即备份”不受影响，待决议的备份也不会被保留策略挤出），每次运行至多提醒一次；“备份与还原”页同时披露：自定义目录中的快照不会被自动提议，需手动从“本机快照”恢复。
- **新增韩语界面。** 界面、安装器与开屏引导新增韩语，与既有 12 种语言并列；同时对 1.3.8 时期机翻事故（es/ar/ru/hi/bn/fr 等）做全量译文修正。
- **更从容的更新体验。** 每日墙钟锚点检查+失败退避、可点的更新 Toast 按钮、更新后自动弹更新内容、后台下载（跳过计费网络+就绪 Toast）、全程状态徽章、更新日志可按版本浏览完整发布历史（所有机器一致、看过的离线可读）与设置窗活动浮层；后台下载与更新通知独立开关。
- **全提权桌面的清晰告知。** 整个桌面以管理员权限运行时（UAC 关闭/内置 Administrator/公司管理）一次性弹窗说明拖出限制与确切恢复路径（专业版 secpol、家庭版注册表、标准账户），附可复制命令与 toast 兜底；拖放诊断同步归因，确认后不再打扰。
- **托盘图标可以隐藏。** 「隐藏」样式：启动即不创建、运行时切换即时生效、任务栏重建不复活。
- **Windows 11 原生右键菜单。** 单竖列行内图标、资源管理器经典序、直出毛玻璃；「打开方式」列真实关联应用（含 UWP 带图标）；空白处「新建」「查看」「排序」子菜单；原生 ZIP 压缩（tar 变体、多选）与解压。命名空间快捷方式（此电脑/回收站/控制面板）继承目标动词——回收站快捷方式可直接「清空回收站」（已空时置灰），全部条目新增「创建快捷方式」。
- **格内回收站快捷方式成为删除投放目标。** 从桌面、资源管理器或其它格子拖文件到回收站快捷方式上即移入回收站（确认框与 Windows 一致），投放或清空后图标即时刷新。
- **Shell 位置拖入格子即建快捷方式。** 回收站、此电脑等拖到格子上直接创建可用快捷方式；来源无内容或正忙时给出 toast 提示。
- **开屏引导全新改版。** 三块拼片 logo 组装动画开场，文件投放场景演示，展示真实托管存储路径及移动/粘贴提示。
- **更稳的格子。** 位置扛住显示器拓扑变化（过期快照不再覆盖用户最新摆放），贴边长条不再跳角，分组胶囊切标签后即时展开，展开最小尺寸减半至 25 DIP，隐藏后马上唤出不再卡顿（隐藏后修剪延迟并跳过小额）。
- **Windows 11 25H2 颜色子菜单闪退修复。** 待办格子右键「颜色标记」不再闪退：未选中色点改为无字体纯几何绘制，绕开 25H2 删除字体文件却残留注册表项、导致菜单首次度量即致命失败的问题。
