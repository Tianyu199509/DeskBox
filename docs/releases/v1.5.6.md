# DeskBox 1.5.6

DeskBox 1.5.6 is a big multiscreen, appearance, and layout release. Widgets now belong to the display they sit on, and every combination of connected displays remembers its own arrangement — unplug a screen and its widgets move over (or collapse into capsules) after a short grace period, plug it back in and everything returns to its exact spot, and first-seen combinations seed sensibly instead of piling onto the primary; Windows-side changes (primary switches, resolution and scale round-trips, sleep and lock re-enumerations, full-screen games, late docks) no longer shuffle widgets. Widgets can dress up: per-widget icon swapping (emoji or image), per-widget background images with fit and dim controls, border styles, three global background modes including a panorama where each widget frames its own slice of the image by its position on the desktop, and a text-shadow toggle. A new Displays page pins widgets and groups to a display by hardware identity — surviving sleep, reconnects, and display renumbering — with an arrangement preview, one-click identify, pin-all, and a clearable default display for new widgets. Elevated launches explain themselves: running as administrator blocks drag and drop with Explorer, so DeskBox now notices, explains, and offers a one-click restart at normal privileges (autostart launches restart themselves and clear the RUNASADMIN compatibility flag). The settings window got an information-architecture pass (accordions, reordered pages, Windows-style slide transitions, whole-row entry cards, readable native notice bars), and the direct-edition uninstaller guards data when the Store edition may still be installed. It also adds a press-to-record hotkey editor, silent startup, a copy-first managed-storage migration, three new reveal effects plus Spring easing and staggered timing, a configurable auto-organize dwell, a weather icon-style picker with four bundled vector sets, drag-out modifier-key and result hints, and a run of feedback-driven fixes: Windows 10 drag-outs without Explorer's operation picker, tray double-click replay, a compact capsule stuck expanded, clipboard images from WeChat/QQ that standard decoding rejected, and an unexplained empty search state while Everything is not connected. Platform: Windows App Runtime 2.5.1, and the Windows 10 floor drops to build 19041 (version 2004).

Release date: 2026-10-02 (content prepared from `main` at `1542fcd8` — the 1.5.6 code batch, PR #495 — plus the display-binding, multiscreen-behavior v2, elevation-launch-gate, WinAppSDK 2.5.1 / Windows-10-floor, settings-window reorganization, silent-startup, tray crossing-fade, uninstaller-guard, touch-pointer, and hotkey-recorder batches; final date to be set when publication is authorized).

## Highlights

- **Widgets belong to their display.** Dragging a widget to a screen makes that screen its home. Every combination of connected displays keeps its own arrangement; unplugging a display moves its widgets to a remaining screen after a short grace period (sized proportionally, or collapsed into capsules if you set it so), and replugging returns every widget to its exact spot. A combination seen for the first time seeds from the most similar previous arrangement instead of dumping everything onto the primary.
- **Windows no longer shuffles your widgets.** Switching the primary display, changing resolution or scale, rearranging monitors, lock-screen and sleep re-enumerations, and full-screen games that temporarily change the resolution all leave widgets where they are. Startup waits out late-arriving dock and DisplayLink displays instead of crowding every widget onto the laptop screen and scattering them again.
- **Elevated launches explain themselves.** Elevated DeskBox cannot accept drags from Explorer — a Windows security boundary — so an elevated launch now shows a notice with a one-click "restart with normal privileges" button, or a plain explanation when the machine has no standard-user context to relaunch into (UAC off). Autostart launches restart themselves automatically, guarded against loops, and the compatibility flag that keeps relaunching DeskBox elevated is cleared along the way.
- **Record your own hotkeys.** The global and search hotkey cards gain a press-to-record editor: any Win / Ctrl / Alt / Shift combination, or a lone F1–F24 key, becomes the global hotkey (the search hotkey takes everything except Win-key chords), system-reserved chords like Win+V or Alt+Space record because the keys are intercepted for the dialog, chords owned by the other hotkey are rejected outright, risky combinations warn, the presets stay one click away, and the live hotkeys pause while you record.
- **Dress up your widgets.** Every widget can swap its icon for an emoji (categorized picker, recents, random roll) or a local image, set its own background with contain / fill fit and a dim slider, and pick a border style. Global widget backgrounds offer three modes — follow the material, one unified image, or a panorama where each widget frames its own slice of the image by its position on the desktop — and Appearance gains a text-shadow toggle.
- **Pin widgets to a display.** The new Displays page (Settings) shows an arrangement preview with per-screen widget lists, flashes a number on each screen to identify it, pins every widget to the current layout in one click, and sets a clearable default display for new widgets. Widgets and groups also bind from their context menu; bindings follow display hardware identity, so they survive sleep, reconnects, and Windows renumbering displays, and fall back gracefully while the bound display is offline.
- **A tidier Settings window.** Low-frequency settings fold into expandable accordions, pages are reordered to put fundamentals first, section changes slide in and out the way Windows' own Settings do, entry cards are clickable across the whole row even with inline controls, and notice cards render as readable native info bars with a proper severity tint.
- **Run quietly, migrate safely.** Silent startup keeps DeskBox hidden until the first tray click, hotkey, or activation. Moving files into managed storage is now copy-first: preview, per-phase progress (preparing / copying / verifying / committing), resumable retry, and dedicated errors — every file is hash-checked on both sides before the storage path switches, and the originals are never deleted; they stay untouched in the old location.
- **Motion and timing.** Three new reveal effects (Edge scale, Tilt, Wipe), a Spring easing option, and staggered per-widget timing; hiding widgets slides them fully out with a per-window fade as they cross the screen edge; desktop files wait a configurable dwell — real-time to 12 hours — before auto-organizing.
- **Pick a weather icon style.** Settings → Weather gains an icon-style combo with four bundled vector sets: Fluent (Microsoft Fluent Emoji Color, MIT — the Windows 11 emoji look and the new default), DeskBox hand-drawn line-art, Meteocons Flat, and Meteocons Line (MIT). Rendering goes through bundled SVG assets instead of the Segoe UI Emoji font, so Windows 10 and 11 show the same icons.
- **Windows 10 drag-outs without the operation picker.** Ordinary file drags out of a widget no longer surface Explorer's Copy / Move / Cancel chooser, a brief Shift/Ctrl hint shows at the top of the widget while dragging, a toast reports the operation that actually ran, and the "when dragging out of a widget" drop-down in settings lists its options again on Native AOT builds.
- **Tray icon calms down.** A second click inside the double-click window completes quietly instead of replaying the toggle queue and hiding widgets right after the reveal animation (feedback 237). The tray context flyout retries its assignment on early-logon sessions and falls back to the native menu instead of failing startup (feedback 343).
- **Capsule lease fix.** The widget foreground-color picker handoff is now released on every dismissal path — a compact capsule no longer stays expanded forever after the menu closed another way (issue 437).
- **Feedback honesty.** Submissions only start the cooldown when they succeed, and a stale server-side rate limit heals itself by rotating the anonymous client id instead of locking you out (issue 449).
- **Explained empty states.** Search tells you when an all-empty file list is because Everything — the file-name provider — is not connected (feedback 291); empty widgets get a help card spelling out the likely cause and fix.
- **Clipboard images that used to fail.** Quick Capture falls back to the raw PNG / CF_DIB payloads when the standard bitmap channel cannot decode the image, so captures from WeChat, QQ, and browsers land instead of reporting "empty or unsupported" (feedback 335).
- **Managed-storage guards.** Renaming a widget's managed folder retries briefly against transient handles (antivirus, indexer) and reports a clear message when a persistent lock — like an Explorer window inside the folder — remains (feedback 240). Choosing the desktop or a desktop subfolder as the storage root is rejected up front, and network / removable / system-drive roots warn before they are accepted.

### Also fixed (user-visible)

- Native AOT builds: the cloud-backup page's password box resolves correctly again — "Test connection" and "Save" no longer throw "Specified cast is not valid" (incident P1-1, rooted via DynamicDependency plus an AOT deep-smoke regression check).
- The music widget no longer throws on stale session reads when a player exits or churns mid-refresh — the recurring timeline exception noise in diagnostics is gone.
- Diagnostics exports keep HRESULT hex error codes readable instead of redacting them as secrets.
- The global hotkey and search hotkey cards explain that hotkeys cannot reach DeskBox while the focused window runs elevated — a Windows security limitation, not a DeskBox fault.
- Microsoft Store edition: backup restores no longer fail at the relaunch step — the restart now goes through the packaged-app restart agent instead of the direct-install-only updater helper.

### 主要更新

- **格子归属显示器。** 把格子拖到哪块屏，那块屏就是它的归属；每种显示器组合各自记住摆放。拔屏后经短暂宽限移到剩余屏（按比例缩放，或收起为胶囊），接回时逐格精确归位；首次出现的组合按最相似既往布局播种。
- **Windows 不再打乱你的格子。** 切换主显示器、改分辨率缩放、重排显示器、锁屏睡眠重枚举、全屏游戏临时改分辨率——格子都不动；启动等待晚到的扩展坞/DisplayLink 屏，不再先挤到笔记本再散开。
- **管理员运行有说法了。** 提权启动弹窗说明拖拽受限（Windows 安全边界），一键以普通权限重启；UAC 关闭等无法降权的机器给出解释；自启提权实例自动重启（带循环断路器），并清除反复提权的兼容性标记。
- **快捷键可以自己录了。** 全局与搜索快捷键卡片新增按键录制编辑器：Win/Ctrl/Alt/Shift 任意组合或单独的 F1–F24 即可设为全局快捷键（搜索快捷键除 Win 键组合外均可），Win+V、Alt+Space 等系统预留组合因录制期按键被拦截也能录入；与另一个快捷键冲突的组合直接拒绝、高风险组合先行提醒，预设一键可选，录制期间现有热键暂停触发。
- **装扮你的格子。** 任意格子可换表情图标（分类选择器、最近使用、随机）或本地图片，设置自己的背景图（包含/填充契合度+明暗调节）与边框样式。全局格子背景三模式——跟随材质、统一图片、按各格桌面位置取景的全景图片；外观新增文字阴影开关。
- **把格子钉在指定显示器上。** 新的「显示器」设置页带排列预览与逐屏格子清单，一键识别（各屏闪现编号）、一键固定当前布局、可清除的新格子默认屏。格子与格子组也可从右键菜单绑定；绑定跟随显示器硬件身份，睡眠、重插、Windows 重排编号都不受影响，绑定屏不在线时自动回落。
- **更清爽的设置窗口。** 低频设置收进风琴卡，页面按基础优先重排，分区切换带 Windows 设置式左右滑入转场，入口卡整行可点（含内联控件），提示条改用带提示色、清晰可读的原生信息条。
- **安静启动，安全迁移。** 静默启动让 DeskBox 一直隐藏到第一次托盘点击、热键或激活。文件迁入受管存储改为先复制：预览、分阶段进度（准备/复制/校验/提交）、可恢复重试与专属错误——每个文件都经双侧哈希校验通过后才会切换收纳路径；源文件全程保留在原位置，软件绝不自动删除。
- **动效与时机。** 三个新显示效果（边缘缩放、倾斜、擦除）、弹簧缓动与逐格错峰时序；隐藏格子完整滑出并在跨屏边缘逐窗渐隐；桌面文件可配置停留多久后才被自动整理（实时到 12 小时）。
- **天气图标风格选择。** 设置 → 天气新增图标风格下拉：Fluent（微软 Fluent Emoji 彩色版，MIT，Windows 11 观感，新默认）、DeskBox 手绘简笔、Meteocons 扁平、Meteocons 线框（MIT）。渲染改走内置 SVG 资产而非 Segoe UI Emoji 字体，双系统显示一致。
- **Windows 10 拖出不再弹选择框。** 普通左键拖出不再触发 Explorer 的"复制 / 移动 / 取消"操作选择器，拖出时格子顶部短暂显示 Shift/Ctrl 修饰键提示，松手后弹出实际执行操作的结果提示；设置里"拖出格子时"下拉在 Native AOT 版恢复显示选项。
- **托盘图标安静下来。** 双击窗口内的第二次点击静默完成，不再重放切换队列把刚展开的格子藏起来（反馈 237）。早期登录会话中托盘右键菜单赋值失败时按节奏重试，并回退原生菜单而不再启动失败（反馈 343）。
- **胶囊租约修复。** 格子前景色取色器的交接在所有关闭路径上都会释放——紧凑胶囊不再永久保持展开（issue 437）。
- **反馈诚实。** 提交只有成功后才计入冷却；服务端残留的限流状态通过轮换匿名客户端 ID 自愈，不再把你永久锁在门外（issue 449）。
- **有解释的空状态。** 文件名搜索提供方 Everything 未连接时，全空的文件列表会给出说明（反馈 291）；空格子带帮助卡，写明可能原因与处理办法。
- **以前接不住的剪贴板图片。** 标准位图通道解码失败时，随记回退读取原始 PNG / CF_DIB 数据——微信、QQ、浏览器的截图现在能正常进来（反馈 335）。
- **受管存储守卫。** 重命名格子受管文件夹会对瞬时占用（杀毒、索引器）短暂重试，遇到长驻占用（如 Explorer 窗口停在文件夹内）给出明确提示（反馈 240）。把桌面或其子文件夹选作收纳根会被提前拒绝；网络、可移动磁盘、系统盘根目录在接受前会给出预警。
- **商店版还原修复。** 备份还原不再在重启环节失败——重启改走打包应用重启代理，不再依赖直装版专属的更新器进程（1.5.5 商店版即带此病）。
